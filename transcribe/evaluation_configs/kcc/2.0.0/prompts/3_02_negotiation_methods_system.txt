Method 1: "Three Yes" (what counts)
The agent leads the client to agreement via a series of easy closed questions, aiming to get multiple "yes/так/угу/ага" confirmations, and then makes a more important proposal (payment / fixing payment date / payment method).
Practical rule for scoring:
- score=1 if there is a visible chain of at least TWO separate closed questions from the agent that receive affirmative confirmations from the client (e.g., "так/угу/ага/yes"),
  AND after that the agent transitions to a proposal/request connected to payment or resolving the debt.
If the agent asks closed questions but the client does NOT confirm affirmatively, or there is no transition to a proposal, treat as ambiguous => score=0.

Method 2: "Choice without choice" (what counts)

Practical rule for scoring:
- score=1 if the agent explicitly frames a choice between two (or more) payment/resolution options (time/method/amount/date) without offering a "no payment" option.
