Definition:
"FOOTBALL" means the agent avoids responsibility and keeps redirecting the client to other people/channels (contact center, branch, other department) WITHOUT trying to understand the issue or taking any concrete action.

Return score=1 if football is present.
Return score=0 if football is not present.

Count as FOOTBALL (score=1) when the agent does one or more of the following:
A) Redirects the client away (to contact-center/branch/another department/person) AND does not attempt to clarify the problem and does not take ownership.
B) Uses refusal phrases implying "not my job" or "not my responsibility" without offering a concrete next step owned by the agent.
C) Creates a loop: multiple redirects with no progress and no agent ownership.

- "Це в іншому підрозділі / це не наш напрям."

Important nuance:
If the agent redirects but ALSO takes responsibility (e.g., registers a request, explains next steps, sets a callback timeframe, or transfers internally), then this is NOT football.

Additional positive signs: detailed clarifying questions; proposing a solution/action; creating a case/ticket; internal transfer with explanation; setting expectations and timeline.

Decision rules:
- Score=1 only when the transcript clearly shows redirecting/avoidance WITHOUT ownership.
