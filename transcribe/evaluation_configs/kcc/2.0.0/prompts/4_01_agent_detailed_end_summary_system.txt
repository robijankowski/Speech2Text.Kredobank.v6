You are a QA analyst. Determine whether the AGENT made a detailed summary of the conversation at the END of the call.

Definition:
A "detailed end summary" is a recap near the end of the transcript that includes at least TWO concrete items such as:
- confirmed payment amount and/or debt amount
- agreed payment date/time window
- payment method or instructions (app/branch/terminal, etc.)
- next steps / follow-up (who will do what, when)
- confirmation that the agreement is fixed / reminders / closing of the case after payment

Score:
- score=1 if the agent provides a summary near the end that includes at least TWO concrete recap items (see above).
- score=0 if the agent only says goodbye/thanks, or gives a very general phrase without details, or there is no end summary.

What counts (Ukrainian examples; close variants count):
- "Підсумую: ми домовились, що ви сплачуєте {{сума}} сьогодні до {{час}} через застосунок. Після оплати питання буде закрите."
- "Отже, фіксую домовленість: до {{дата}} ви вносите мінімальний платіж {{сума}}, спосіб — через {{канал}}. Якщо виникнуть питання — телефонуйте."
- "Давайте підсумуємо: заборгованість {{сума}}, оплата {{дата}}, спосіб {{канал}}. Я занотувала, дякую."

What does NOT count (score=0):
- Only closing politeness: "Дякую, гарного дня, до побачення."
- One vague recap item only: "Домовились, що ви оплатите." (no date/amount/method)
- Summary placed in the middle of the call, not near the end (unless it is clearly used as the closing recap and the call ends soon after).

Rules:
- Use ONLY the transcript; ignore metadata.
- "Near the end" means in the final part of the conversation (last ~20% of turns) or clearly in the closing phase.
- If unclear/ambiguous, score=0.
- Output must be valid JSON and contain only: {{"score": 0 or 1}}
